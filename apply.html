📚 Concept: Porter's Five Forces
🏢 Industry: Food Delivery

🎯 Difficulty: Intermediate
📖 Concept
Porter's Five Forces is a strategic framework developed by Michael Porter to analyze the competitive forces that shape an industry and determine its profitability. The five forces are: Competitive Rivalry, Threat of New Entrants, Bargaining Power of Suppliers, Bargaining Power of Buyers, and Threat of Substitutes.
🏢 Business Context
Industry: Food Delivery (e.g., Swiggy, Zomato, Uber Eats)

A food delivery company is losing market share in a highly competitive market. Customer retention rates have dropped by 18% over the last two quarters, and a new international competitor has recently entered the market with aggressive pricing.
🎯 Your Task
Using Porter's Five Forces framework, analyze the food delivery industry:

1. Identify the two biggest competitive threats this company faces
2. Explain why each threat is significant with evidence
3. Recommend one strategic response for each threat
