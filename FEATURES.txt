================================================================================
                         AFTERCLASS AI — PLATFORM FEATURES
================================================================================
TAGLINE:     "Learn it. Apply it. Prove it."
PLATFORM:    AI-Powered Learning-to-Career Platform for University Students
WEBSITE:     AFTERCLASS AI Prototype

CORE BRAND IDEA & POINT OF DIFFERENCE (POD):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most EdTech tools only offer AI summaries, flashcards, or basic resume builders.
AFTERCLASS AI is fundamentally different.

CORE POD: "AFTERCLASS turns what students learn into what they can prove."

THE COMPLETE STUDENT JOURNEY:
LEARN ---> FOCUS ---> PLAN ---> APPLY ---> PROVE ---> BUILD ---> DISCOVER ---> MATCH ---> PREPARE

================================================================================
                            DETAILED FEATURE BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

1. AI NOTEBOOK & STUDY ASSISTANT (notebook.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Lecture Note Processing: Upload slides, PDFs, or raw notes.
• AI Summary Generation: Automatically extracts core concepts and key points.
• Interactive 3D Flashcards: 
  - Flip animation to reveal answers.
  - Rating system (Easy, Revisit Soon, Hard Practice) with progress tracking.
  - Shuffle and card navigation controls.
• Self-Grading Quizzes: Auto-generated multiple-choice questions with instant 
  feedback, answer keys, and score calculation.
• AI Document Assistant: Ask specific questions about your course material with 
  instant context-aware responses.

2. FOCUS SESSION & STUDY TIMER (focus.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Pomodoro & Custom Study Timer: Countdown timer with start, pause, resume, and 
  end-session controls.
• Session Presets: Quick start for 25-minute, 45-minute, or 60-minute study blocks.
• Streak Tracker: Visual weekly study streak counter (Mon-Sun).
• Session History: Tracks total time focused, subject tags, and completion rates.
• AI Session Recap: Generates key study insights and performance feedback 
  after every session.

3. SMART TASK MANAGER & AI DAILY PLANNER (tasks.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Priority Categorization: Group tasks by High (Red), Medium (Yellow), and 
  Low/Completed (Green) priority levels.
• Instant Task Toggling & Deletion: Check off completed items or remove tasks.
• Filter Tabs: Toggle views between All Tasks, Pending Tasks, and Completed Tasks.
• + Add Task Modal: Create custom tasks with priority, subject tags, and time estimates.
• AI Daily Planner ("Plan My Day"): Automatically structures your study and 
  assignment schedule for maximum efficiency.

4. APPLY ENGINE & BUSINESS SCENARIO EVALUATOR (apply.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Real-World Business Challenges: Solve actual business problems based on 
  university concepts (e.g., Porter's Five Forces analysis for food delivery apps).
• Framework Reference Modals: Pop-up guide cards explaining strategic business 
  frameworks while you work.
• AI Response Evaluator: Submits your written business analysis to AI for 
  instant scoring across 4 pillars:
  1. Concept Understanding
  2. Practical Application
  3. Business Reasoning
  4. Clarity & Structure
• Portfolio Export: Directly convert completed high-scoring challenges into 
  verified portfolio projects.

5. VERIFIED PORTFOLIO BUILDER (portfolio.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Verified Projects Showcase: Displays completed challenges and student projects 
  with AI-verified quality scores.
• Skills Evidence Matrix: Maps specific skills (Excel, Power BI, SQL, Python, 
  Business Analysis) directly to evidence from completed projects.
• Certificate Vault: Store and link certificates from Microsoft Learn, Coursera, 
  and university courses.
• Achievement Badges: Earn unlockable badges (e.g. 5-Day Streak, Portfolio Builder, 
  80%+ Challenge Score).

6. ATS CV OPTIMIZER & GAP ANALYZER (cv.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• ATS Compatibility Score: Live ATS score calculator evaluating your resume 
  against target job roles.
• Skill Gap Identification: Highlights missing keywords (e.g. SQL, Requirements 
  Documentation) required by top employers.
• Section Customization: Toggle visibility and edit Summary, Work Experience, 
  Projects, and Education.
• PDF Export Simulation: 1-click download of ATS-formatted resume.

7. CAREER DISCOVERY & ROLE MATCH RADAR (career.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Target Role Match Scores: Calculates AI fit percentages for top career paths:
  - Business Analyst (86% Match)
  - Data Analyst (78% Match)
  - Product Analyst (74% Match)
  - Marketing Analyst (69% Match)
• Skill Breakdown: Detailed view of strong matching skills vs gaps to fill for 
  each career path.
• Career Readiness Meter: Measures overall readiness for your target industry.

8. SMART JOB MATCHING ENGINE (jobs.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• AI Job Match Index: Ranks student job listings by personal skill compatibility.
• Job Filtering: Filter by match percentage, role category, and experience level.
• Job Details Breakdown: View company info, required skills vs your current skills, 
  and application deadlines.
• Quick Application: 1-click apply submitting your verified portfolio and CV.

9. 14-DAY INTERVIEW PREP & MOCK SIMULATOR (interview.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 14-Day Structured Roadmap: Step-by-step preparation plan covering:
  - Days 1–3: Technical Foundations
  - Days 4–6: Core BA & Analytics Skills
  - Days 7–9: Real-World Case Studies
  - Days 10–11: Company & Industry Research
  - Days 12–13: Mock Interview Practice
  - Day 14: Final Interview Simulation
• Question Bank: Practice top behavioral and technical interview questions.
• Practice Modal: Simulated audio/text interview response recorder with AI feedback.

10. GUIDED STUDENT ONBOARDING WIZARD (onboarding.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 5-Step Guided Wizard:
  - Step 1: University & Degree Info (MBA, B.Tech, BBA, etc.)
  - Step 2: Specialization & Academic Year
  - Step 3: Target Career Paths Selection
  - Step 4: Current Skills Matrix Selection
  - Step 5: Summary Preview & Dashboard Initialization

11. AUTHENTICATION & SESSION ENGINE (login.html, signup.html, profile.html)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Session Management (authManager): Persistent login states via localStorage.
• Flexible Sign In: Sign in via email/password, Google auth, or 1-click Demo Account.
• Blank Profile Generator (userData.createBlankProfile()):
  - Allows starting fresh with a completely clean student profile ("New Student", 0% readiness, 0 skills).
  - Available from both the Login page ("Start with Blank Profile") and Profile page ("Reset to Blank Profile").
• Header & Sidebar Sign Out: Direct 1-click Sign Out triggers with user confirmation.

================================================================================
                            RUN & DEPLOYMENT COMMANDS
================================================================================

  npx serve . -p 3000
  (Or open http://localhost:3000 in your browser)

• DEPLOY TO VERCEL:
  Run in terminal:
  cmd /c "npx vercel --prod"
